# Adventure Hub (TravelSense) - User Guide

Welcome to your ultimate travel companion! This guide will explain how to use the various features of the application, especially the unique "WOW-factor" additions.

---

## 🌎 1. Dashboard & The 3D Travel Globe
**Purpose**: A high-level overview of your trip's finances, weather, and locations.

* **How to use it**: When you open the app, the first thing you see is the **Interactive 3D Travel Globe**. 
  * It uses a high-resolution daytime satellite texture (similar to Google Earth).
  * You can click and drag the globe to spin it, and use your mouse wheel to zoom in and out.
  * The glowing blue pins represent your past and upcoming travel destinations.
* **Insights**: Below the globe, you will see your total budget, weather for your current destination, and an AI-driven Copilot forecast that predicts if you're going to overspend based on your daily average.

---

## 💳 2. Expense Tracker & AI Receipt Scanner
**Purpose**: Log daily spending and seamlessly split bills.

* **How to use it**: Navigate to the **Expenses** tab in the sidebar. You can manually add expenses by clicking "Log Expense".
* **Unique Feature: AI Scanner**:
  1. Click the **"AI Scanner"** button at the top.
  2. Upload a photo of a restaurant receipt.
  3. The app will use Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to extract the line items and prices.
  4. A list will appear. Simply **tap the items** that you personally consumed.
  5. The app will automatically calculate your exact share and add it to your expense log!

---

## 📅 3. Swipe-to-Decide Itinerary Builder
**Purpose**: Democratize group trip planning so everyone gets a say in what you do.

* **How to use it**: Navigate to the **Itinerary** tab.
* **Unique Feature: Swipe-to-Decide**:
  1. Click the **"Build Group Itinerary"** button.
  2. A Tinder-style swiping interface will appear, pulling real locations (beaches, cafes, monuments) using the OpenStreetMap API.
  3. **Swipe Right** if you want to visit a place, or **Swipe Left** to pass.
  4. Once you finish swiping, the app will simulate matching your preferences with your friends' preferences and instantly generate an optimized daily schedule based on your matches!

---

## 🗺️ 4. Live Map & Group Tracking
**Purpose**: Never lose a group member in a crowded city again.

* **How to use it**: Navigate to the **Live Map** tab.
* **Features**: You'll see a real-time map centered on your location. Other group members will appear as pulsing avatars on the map. You can also toggle "Attractions", "Food", and "Hotels" to instantly populate the map with nearby points of interest.

---

## 🛡️ 5. Safety Vault
**Purpose**: Securely store vital documents and trigger emergency alerts.

* **How to use it**: Navigate to the **Safety** tab.
* **Encrypted Vault**: Upload copies of your passport, visas, or insurance. The app encrypts these files locally in your browser using AES encryption so they remain completely private.
* **SOS Button**: In an emergency, tapping the large red SOS button will broadcast your live GPS coordinates to your travel group.

---

## ⚙️ 6. Profile & Settings
**Purpose**: Customize your experience.

* **How to use it**: Navigate to **Profile** or **Settings**. Here you can change your display currency, update your password, or change the app's theme. 
* **Note on Saving**: Even without a database running, clicking "Save" will persistently store your preferences in your browser's local storage so your settings are remembered next time you open the app!
